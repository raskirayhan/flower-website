# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Contributors are expected to be respectful, constructive, and inclusive. Keep feedback focused on the page, code, documentation, or user experience rather than on individuals.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and sharing private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the maintainer through the [GitHub profile](https://github.com/raskirayhan), with enough context to review the incident.
