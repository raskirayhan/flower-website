Since 2018

Rooted in Passion, Blossoming with Care

Flower Market started as a humble glasshouse greenhouse dedicated to organic botanical horticulture. Today, we deliver nature's purest calm across thousands of homes.

Our Philosophy

Connecting People to Nature's Quiet Wonder

In our fast-paced modern world, the living presence of flowers and foliage is more than mere decoration—it is restorative self-care. Every stem we cut and every potted houseplant we nurture is conditioned with organic bio-stimulants rather than synthetic toxins.

We collaborate with family-owned growers in the volcanic highlands of Ecuador, historical Dutch greenhouses, and local California coastal nurseries.

Our Guarantees

The Three Pillars of Flower Market

Artisan Hand-Tied Precision

No automated factory assembly lines. Every bouquet is hand-selected, cleaned, spiraled, and tied by certified master florists.

Planet First Packaging

We eradicated plastic wrap and synthetic floral foam. Our boxes are 100% recycled paper and our hydration bags are seaweed cellulose.

If your flowers do not flourish for at least seven days in fresh water, we immediately send a free replacement bouquet.
