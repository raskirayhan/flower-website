Privacy Policy & Store Guarantees

Last Updated: October 24, 2026

1. 7-Day Bloom Freshness Guarantee

Flower Market guarantees that all cut flower bouquets will remain vibrant and fragrant for a minimum of 7 consecutive days from the date of courier delivery, provided the included care instructions (water change every 48h, 45° underwater cut) are followed. If your blooms wilt prematurely, submit a photo to hello@flowermarket.shop within 7 days for an immediate 100% complimentary replacement.

2. Information We Collect

We collect recipient names, delivery addresses, email addresses, and contact phone numbers solely for order fulfillment, courier transit SMS notifications, and our opt-in Floral Club newsletter. We never sell or lease customer personal information to third-party advertisers.

3. Secure Payment & Encryption

All transactions are processed through end-to-end 256-bit SSL encrypted channels conforming to PCI-DSS Level 1 standards. Credit card details are never retained on our local application servers.

4. Sustainable Packaging Pledge

Flower Market pledges that 100% of our wrapping paper, boxes, and transit insulation are derived from certified post-consumer recycled or compostable botanical sources.
